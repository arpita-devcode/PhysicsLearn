V = I × R
🔋 ─── Ω ─── 💡

📘 1. Understand the Concept

Ohm's Law states that the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage and inversely proportional to the resistance, provided the temperature remains constant.

V = I × R
  • V = Voltage (volts)
  • I = Current (amperes)
  • R = Resistance (ohms)

💡 Example

If V = 12 V and R = 6 Ω:

I = V / R = 12 / 6 = 2 A
